1 Atkinson Cycle Extended Expansion Ganesan Eq. 2.73, p. 66

Expansion ratio e = 13.5:1 with effective compression r = 10.2:1 via Late Intake Valve Closing (LIVC). Air-standard efficiency reaches 63.0% (vs 60.2% Otto), extracting maximum work from expansion.

2 Toroidal Squish Combustion Chamber Bowl Sec. 11.18.1, Fig. 11.19d & Sec. 20.6.1

70% squish quench area with central aerodynamic deflector pip creates a high-velocity smoke-ring vortex in piston bowl for stable ultra-lean stratified combustion without wall wetting.

3 Swirl Control Valves (SCV) in Dual-Path Runners Sec. 20.8.6, Fig. 20.10, p. 678

Electronic stepper shuts secondary runner during cruising to force air through helical swirl port, generating intense micro-turbulent swirl (A/F up to 22:1) to accelerate flame speed and avert lean misfire.

4 Liquid-Cooled Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) Sec. 14.18, pp. 443-445, Fig. 14.11

Finned 316L stainless heat exchanger cools recirculated gas to 120°C, acting as a thermal diluent to lower peak flame temperature from 2450 K to 2120 K, eliminating engine knock and cutting raw NOx by 65%.

5 Unthrottled VVA Pumping Loss Elimination Sec. 20.6.1, p. 668 & Sec. 4.5.1, p. 139

Variable Valve Actuation regulates intake charge volume directly by valve closing point without throttle butterfly plate restriction, dropping pumping mean effective pressure (pmep) from 0.85 bar to 0.07 bar.

6 Low-Friction Slipper Skirt & Hydrodynamic Lubrication Ch. 12, pp. 361-387, Fig. 12.6

Graphite-coated slipper skirts with reduced contact area and low-tension DLC rings reduce mechanical friction (mmep) by 20% while 0W-20 multi-grade synthetic oil maintains full-film hydrodynamic regime (νN/p ≥ 0.008).

7 Inlet-Valve Mach Index Control (Z ≤ 0.55) Sec. 1.8.10, Eq. 1.18 & Fig. 1.16

Maintains inlet Mach Index Z = (b/Di)²(Vp/(Ci·α)) ≤ 0.55 across cruising range, avoiding choking and ensuring peak volumetric efficiency ηv ≥ 88%.

8 Closed-Loop Heated Lambda & Dual Knock Sensors Sec. 17.7, pp. 592-593, Fig. 17.24

Heated Zirconia λ-sensor with sub-50ms response provides closed-loop AFR regulation while dual piezoelectric knock sensors advance MBT timing to target peak cylinder pressure at 12° aTDC.

A Auxiliary Parasitic Work (amep) Ganesan Sec. 12.2, p. 362

The serpentine belt transfers mechanical work from the crankshaft to vital subsystems (water pump, alternator, A/C compressor). Consumes 0.25 to 0.45 bar AMEP, reducing indicated power to brake power (bp = ip - fp).

B Torsional Harmonic Balancing (TVD) Ganesan Ch. 18, p. 542

The crankshaft driving pulley is an elastomeric Torsional Vibration Damper. It damps 6th and 12th harmonic resonance spikes from the V12 60° firing pulses by 91%, isolating the belt from high-frequency torsional twist.

C Euler-Eytelwein Friction & Wrap Angle Euler's Capstan Law

Multi-ribbed 40° V-grooves increase effective friction (μ/sin 20° ≈ 1.11). Strategic guide idlers maintain wrap angles >140°, preventing belt slip under sudden acceleration without excessive static bearing pre-loads.

D Dynamic Hydraulic Tensioning Accessory Stability

A spring-loaded swing arm with hydraulic damping maintains 520 N constant preload across thermal expansion ranges (-40°C to +125°C), eliminating high-RPM belt flap and premature cord fatigue.
